# "The Last Escape": script v1 (2026-11-13)

Format: cartoon-3d-short, iteration 8. Vertical, English, about 65-70 s, with a directed expressive narrator (voice B,
British and deep, the Kokoro bm_george reference cloned in Chatterbox).

**Why now:** 31 October 2026 was the 100th anniversary of Houdini's death. On Halloween, about 25 séances across
the US tried to reach him, three of them at his old house in New York. A new book this autumn says the official cause
and date of death are wrong.

## Facts and sources
- **Centennial séances.** Wild About Harry counted 25 Houdini séance events on Halloween 2026. Three of them were at
  278 (his New York home), the first at 1:26 pm, the hour he died.
  - [wildabouthoudini.com](https://www.wildabouthoudini.com/)
  - [NPR/WYPR: 100 years later, Houdini's death still haunts Detroit](https://www.wypr.org/wypr-arts/2026-10-31/100-years-later-harry-houdinis-death-still-haunts-detroit)
- **Escapes.** He escaped handcuffs, police cells and, in Boston, the belly of a dead whale that washed ashore
  (Wikipedia).
- **Chinese Water Torture Cell** (from 1912). He was hung upside down by the ankles in a locked, water-filled glass
  and steel tank and held his breath for more than three minutes (Wikipedia).
- **Crashing séances.** He went to séances in disguise, with a reporter and a police officer, and exposed the
  mediums (Wikipedia).
- **Arthur Conan Doyle**, the creator of Sherlock Holmes, believed Houdini was a powerful medium who did his escapes
  with paranormal powers. Their friendship became a public feud (Wikipedia).
- **The punch.** On 22 October 1926, in his dressing room at the Princess Theatre in Montreal, McGill student
  J. Gordon Whitehead struck him in the abdomen several times (Wikipedia).
- **The show went on.** He refused surgery for acute appendicitis and performed in Detroit on 24 October with a
  fever of 104 °F (40 °C) (Wikipedia).
- **Death.** He died of peritonitis on 31 October 1926 (Halloween) in Detroit's Grace Hospital, aged 52.
- **The new book (2026).** Larrian Gillespie's *Houdini: The Man Who Died Twice* argues that he died on 30 October of
  an intestinal blockage, which she links to a doctor letting him eat vegetables and sour cream after surgery. No
  autopsy was done. Scholars are split.
  - [AP via NY1, 31 Oct 2026](https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/ap-top-news/2026/10/31/houdinis-centennial-spotlight-a-newly-published-essay-and-a-fresh-fight-over-when-and-how-he-died)
- **The code.** Houdini and his wife Bess agreed on a secret message, "Rosabelle, believe", after their song. A medium
  who said it would prove real contact (Wikipedia).
- **Ten years.** Bess held a séance every Halloween for ten years. After the last one, in 1936 on the roof of the
  Knickerbocker Hotel in Los Angeles, she put out the candle she had kept burning by his photo. In 1943 she said "ten
  years is long enough to wait for any man" (Wikipedia).
- **Still going.** Houdini séances are held every Halloween to this day (Wikipedia; Wild About Harry).
- [Wikipedia: Harry Houdini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Houdini)

## Catchy checklist
- [x] **Hook in 2 s:** a paradox. 25 séances, one dead man, and he "ignores" them: a ghost with an attitude.
- [x] **Payload as a punch:** "for a hundred years" (the centennial, news this month).
- [x] **Credibility and wow at 6 s:** handcuffs, jail cells... and a dead whale (the absurd third item).
- [x] **A reveal every 3-5 s:** the tank; crashing séances in disguise with a cop; Sherlock's creator thinks he's
  magic; the punch; the show with a fever; died on Halloween; the sour cream twist (news this year).
- [x] **"You see,"** explanation: why a password beats a fake psychic.
- [x] **Turn:** ten years of séances, then the candle and the line about waiting.
- [x] **Loop and viewer address:** the password said aloud, so the viewer now knows it. "It's a fraud. Or his
  greatest escape." It calls back to the hook (séances) and to the escapes.

## Script
This Halloween, twenty-five séances tried to summon the same dead man.
He's ignored them for a hundred years.
Harry Houdini. He escaped handcuffs, jail cells, and a dead whale.
Upside down in a locked tank of water, he held his breath for three minutes.
But his favourite hobby? Crashing séances. In disguise. With a cop.
Even the man who created Sherlock Holmes swore Houdini had real magic powers.
Then, in 1926, a college student punched him in the stomach.
His appendix burst. He did the show anyway, with a fever of a hundred and four.
He died on Halloween.
Although a new book this year blames something else. Sour cream.
You see, Houdini knew fake psychics would claim to reach him.
So he gave his wife a secret password. Two words. Rosabelle, believe.
Bess held a séance every Halloween, for ten years. Nothing.
Then she blew out his candle, and said: ten years is long enough to wait for any man.
So if anyone ever whispers Rosabelle, believe... it's a fraud. Or his greatest escape.
