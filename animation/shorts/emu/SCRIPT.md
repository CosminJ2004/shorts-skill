# "The War Australia Lost to Birds": script v1 (2026-11-12)

This is the cartoon-3d-short format: 2D cartoon cards in a 3D toon world, about 60 s, vertical, one steady
narrator, in English. Iteration 6. The user asked for a viral idea, quality, visible character movement and something
beautiful, so the story is built around running birds, marching soldiers, a bouncing truck and golden wheat.

## Facts and sources

- **Background.** After WWI, Australia gave discharged soldiers farmland in Western Australia to grow wheat. In the
  Depression, emus (most sources: about 20,000) moved onto the farms around Campion, Chandler and Walgoolan, ate the
  crops and broke fences. The ex-soldier farmers asked Defence Minister Sir George Pearce for machine guns.
  [Wikipedia: Emu War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emu_War), [Britannica: Emu War](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Emu-War)
- **The force.** Major G.P.W. Meredith (Royal Australian Artillery), Sergeant S. McMurray and Gunner J. O'Halloran,
  two Lewis guns and 10,000 rounds. Rain delayed the start to 2 November 1932. (Wikipedia)
- **Day one (2 Nov).** About 50 emus at Campion, out of gun range. (Wikipedia)
- **Day three (4 Nov).** An ambush near a dam as more than 1,000 emus approached; the gun jammed after 12 birds, the
  rest scattered. (Wikipedia)
- **The truck.** A gun mounted on a truck failed: the truck could not keep up with the birds, and the ride was too
  rough to fire. (Wikipedia)
- **Lookouts.** Observers reported that each flock had a leader, a big black-plumed bird "which stands fully six
  feet", that kept watch and warned the others. (Wikipedia)
- **Guerrilla tactics.** Ornithologist Dominic Serventy: the emus' "command had evidently ordered guerrilla tactics",
  splitting into small units. (Wikipedia)
- **Speed.** Emus "can sprint at 48 km/h (30 mph)" and stand up to 1.9 m. [Wikipedia: Emu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emu)
- **The count.** When Meredith was recalled on 10 December he reported 986 kills for 9,860 rounds, exactly 10 rounds
  per bird. (Wikipedia)
- **The quote.** Meredith: "If we had a military division with the bullet-carrying capacity of these birds, it would
  face any army in the world." (Wikipedia; the script drops the word "military")
- **After.** Farmers asked for the army again in 1934, 1943 and 1948; the government refused each time. (Wikipedia)
- **Coat of arms.** The emu "appears as a shield bearer on the Coat of arms of Australia with the red kangaroo."
  (Wikipedia: Emu)

**Jokes, all real facts:** the gun jammed in the ambush; the truck was too slow and too bumpy; the lookout emu; ten
bullets per bird; the army refused three times; the winner is on the coat of arms.

**Kept out:** an often-repeated story of an emu tangled in the truck's steering is not in the sources, so it is not
used. No birds are shown hurt: shots miss, birds scatter.

## Script
In 1932, Australia declared war on birds, with machine guns. And the birds won.
After World War One, Australia gave its soldiers farms in the west, to grow wheat.
Then twenty thousand emus walked in, and ate it.
So the farmers asked the army for help.
The army sent one major, two soldiers, two machine guns, and ten thousand bullets.
Day one, the emus stayed out of range.
Day three, a thousand emus walked straight into an ambush.
Then the gun jammed.
So they put a machine gun on a truck.
Luckily, the truck was fast.
Unluckily, the emus were faster, and the ride was so bumpy, nobody could aim.
Every flock even had a lookout, a tall black emu, watching for soldiers.
You see, a flock is not one target.
Shoot at it, and it bursts into little groups, each running at almost fifty kilometres an hour.
It's like trying to catch spilled marbles with one hand.
By December, the army had fired 9,860 bullets, and counted 986 emus.
Exactly ten bullets per bird.
The major said, "If we had a division with the bullet-carrying capacity of these birds, it would face any army in the world."
The army went home, and refused to come back, three more times.
As for the emus, today one of them holds up Australia's coat of arms.
